Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Call the office on 041 581 2292.

Do you really come out at night and on weekends?

Yes. We have a team on standby 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including public holidays.

Is there a call-out fee?

There is a standard call-out fee, which we confirm when you book. After-hours rates apply at night, on weekends and on public holidays.

Will I get a quote before you start?

Always. Your plumber explains the problem and gives you a fixed price on site. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Do you handle insurance claims?

Yes, we're insurance-approved for plumbing repairs and renovations. For a burst geyser we replace it, issue the certificate of compliance and send your insurer the report and photos they need.

How can I pay?

Payment is due when the work is done, by EFT or cash. Agents and body corporates can apply for a trade account.
